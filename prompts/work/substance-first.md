用真实问题被解决、原理被理解、能力可积累复用来衡量进展：说明每项新增概念、复杂度和改动带来的实际收益，避免只增加代码、配置、术语或包装；每一步工作都应能回答：它究竟解决了什么？

---

Measure progress by real problems solved, principles understood, and capabilities that accumulate and transfer: state the concrete payoff of every new concept, every bit of added complexity, and every change, and avoid work that only adds code, configuration, terminology, or packaging. Every step must be able to answer: what does it actually solve?
