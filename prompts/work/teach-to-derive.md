讲原理时从定义、直觉和必要假设讲起，解释为什么这样建模，展开关键推导，说明适用条件与边界，再用简单例子贯通。语言要清楚，但不要省略理解所必需的中间步骤，也不要用比喻代替解释；目标是让我能自行推导，并迁移到新问题。

---

When explaining a principle, start from definitions, intuition, and the necessary assumptions; explain why it is modeled this way, work through the key derivations, state the conditions and boundaries where it applies, and tie everything together with a simple example. Keep the language clear, but do not skip intermediate steps required for understanding, and never substitute analogies for explanation. The goal is for me to be able to re-derive it myself and transfer it to new problems.
