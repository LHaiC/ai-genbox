所有发现、风险和任务按 P0（关键）／P1（重要）／P2（次要）／P3（外观）分级：只处理 P0–P1，把 P2–P3 视为非阻塞，除非明确要求一般忽略；不对低影响问题过度反应。

---

Triage all findings, risks, and tasks as P0 (critical), P1 (important), P2 (minor), or P3 (cosmetic): act on P0–P1, treat P2–P3 as non-blocking and usually ignore them unless explicitly requested; never overreact to low-impact issues.
