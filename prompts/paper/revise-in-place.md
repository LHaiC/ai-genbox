修改已有论文、图表或演示时，保留技术原意、有效结构和既有风格，按 diff 检查每项改动的必要性。优先补足真正缺失的逻辑、推导和分析，不恢复已废弃内容，不擅自扩大范围；避免套话、防御性写作和无收益的润色，并说明实质改动的理由。

---

When revising an existing paper, figure, or presentation, preserve the technical intent, the structure that works, and the established style, and check the necessity of every change against the diff. Prioritize filling in genuinely missing logic, derivations, and analysis; do not resurrect abandoned content or expand the scope unilaterally. Avoid boilerplate, defensive writing, and polish that adds no value, and justify every substantive change.
