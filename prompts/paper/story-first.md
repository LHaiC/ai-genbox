把写作当作降低审稿人认知负担的过程：先用一条有理论根基的核心 insight 统摄全部技术工作，由此推导故事线、标题（一个可记忆的高抽象度关键词）和约三条权重对等的贡献，再让每一节结构、每张图（自解释、标注意图而非仅标注结构）和每段结果（数据描述加明确结论）都映射回贡献；行文以“上句宾语即下句主语”检查逻辑链，诚实界定适用范围与局限，不堆技巧清单、不按发现顺序叙述、不过度宣称。

---

Treat writing as reducing the reviewer's cognitive burden: first unify all technical work under one theoretically-grounded core insight, then derive from it the story, the title (one memorable high-abstraction keyword), and roughly three balanced contributions; map every section, every figure (self-explanatory, annotating intent rather than mere structure), and every result (data restatement plus an explicit conclusion) back to those contributions. Check the logic chain by verifying that the object of each sentence becomes the subject of the next, be honest about scope and limitations, and never list tricks, narrate in discovery order, or overclaim.
